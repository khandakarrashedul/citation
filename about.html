Biography

Khandakar Rashedul Islam is an Environmental Science graduate with research interests in microplastics, PFAS, biochar-based remediation, water and wastewater treatment, and environmental pollution. His research experience focuses on microplastic contamination in marine and freshwater environments, agricultural soils, and indoor and outdoor environment, as well as the potential risks associated with environmental contaminants.

He completed his bachelor's and master's degrees in environmental science at Jashore University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. During his undergraduate and postgraduate research, he worked on microplastic pollution, biochar applications for soil remediation, adsorption-based pollutant removal, groundwater quality assessment, and heavy metal contamination. He has also worked as a Research Assistant at the EST Laboratory and the Asia Arsenic Network, contributing to environmental sampling, laboratory analysis, and manuscript writing, while leading the research of five undergraduate thesis students.

His research has contributed to 16 peer-reviewed international journal publications and has been involved in government and university funded research projects. He has also participated in national and international scientific conferences, presenting research through oral and poster presentations and contributing research abstracts. In recognition of his research contributions, he received the Vice-Chancellor’s Award for Outstanding Research Contributions in 2025. He has experience with FTIR, SEM, ICP-OES, AAS, and GC-MS. He is now seeking a PhD opportunity to investigate emerging contaminants and develop sustainable approaches to water treatment and environmental remediation.
